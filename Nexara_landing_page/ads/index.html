Nexara — Awareness Ad Sets

Pre-launch ads for Nexara. They lead with the real, current problems of Indian healthcare — no product claims — and position Nexara as the connective layer being built to fix them. Two sets: the narrow adherence focus, and the whole-platform connective-tissue story across all four layers.

Set A · Adherence

The retention layer — why care breaks down after the prescription.

01 · The gap

“Fewer than 1 in 4 Indians with high blood pressure have it under control.” — Lancet Regional Health SEA

02 · The broken chain

“3 in 5 Indians diagnosed with high blood pressure still aren't on treatment.” — NFHS-5

03 · The cost

“$4.5T — India's projected loss to chronic disease before 2030.” — WEF / Harvard

Set B · Nexara as a whole

The connective-tissue story — routing, matching, coordination and continuity over one record. Audience: urban, can-pay Indians who can reach care but still fight a disconnected system.

04 · The right door

“Getting sick is easy. Knowing which doctor to see isn't.” — ~80% specialist shortfall, rural CHCs (MoHFW)

05 · Two minutes

“Your doctor has two minutes.” — India's avg primary-care consult ≈ 2 min (BMJ Open, 2017)

06 · The everyday burden

“101M diabetic, 136M on the edge.” — ICMR-INDIAB, Lancet D&E (2023)

07 · The broken thread

“Every clinic keeps a piece of you. None keep the whole.” — the case for one record (ABDM rationale)

08 · Finding care

“Book dinner in 30 seconds. The right doctor still takes days.” — discovery + booking friction

09 · Manifesto

“Healthcare isn't broken. It's disconnected.” — the connective-tissue thesis

10 · Brand / CTA

“Connecting every link in the care chain.” — the four layers, one record · early access
